# Day 21 — post-ride summary

After an acknowledged ride end, **View your ride summary** opens the signed-in participant's own summary. `GET /v1/history/{rideId}` accepts only an account that participated in that completed ride and only until 90 days after the authoritative end. It returns the viewer's recorded distance, participation duration, elapsed pace in min/km, average speed in km/h, own disconnected recorded trace, sample-quality and tracking gaps, and separately labelled member departure/sharing-stop events through the viewer's departure or ride end. Another member's distance and trail are not returned. No planned route is used as recorded distance.

The server derives the summary from retained, validated location samples each time it is read. Samples are ordered by capture time and identity; only consecutive accurate observations in the same consent epoch, at most 30 seconds apart, and within a plausible movement bound contribute distance or a map line. Participation runs from the later of ride start or join until leave or end, including stops. Zero recorded distance gives unavailable elapsed pace. Late eligible historical uploads change the derived result and `updatedAt` without reopening live sharing. The route preview shows at most the first 2,000 accurate observations, marked partial when necessary; full retained samples still contribute to metrics. Gap details and member events are bounded and labelled if incomplete.

The Day 22 history index and Day 23 photos remain separate milestones. A physical-device review of the summary screen and a long-ride performance measurement remain open; neither is claimed by this handover.
